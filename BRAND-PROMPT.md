# Prompt for Claude Design — the beatass identity

Copy everything below the line into a new Claude Design project.

---

I need a complete visual identity for a product called **beatass** (the site is
beatass.com). Please read the whole brief before drawing anything — the existing
product already has a strong look and the identity has to grow out of it, not
replace it.

## What the product is

beatass lets you say the thing you'd never say. You write an anonymous message to
one person, you take your feelings out on a hand-drawn voodoo doll — punch him,
pin him, set him on fire, or love him — and the site records ten seconds of that
and emails it to them as a short looping animation alongside your words.

It is funny, a little mean, and quietly sincere. The joke is the doll. The point
is the message. The doll is soft and a little cute on purpose — hurting something
that adorable is the tension the whole product runs on. But it is not a prank
tool: someone receiving one should feel seen, not attacked.

Free, no sign-up, one page.

## The look it already has, which you must stay inside

The whole product is a **page torn out of a school exercise book**. Everything is
drawn by hand in pen on ruled paper.

**Colours — these are fixed, please use these exact values:**

| Role | Hex | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#fbf7ea` | the page itself |
| Paper, lighter | `#fffdf5` | input boxes, the message sheet |
| Desk (behind the page) | `#e8e0cc` | the surface the page sits on |
| Ruled lines | `#cddaea` | the faint blue horizontal rules |
| Margin rule | `#e3a8a2` | the red vertical line down the left |
| **Ballpoint blue** | `#26356e` | all text, the doll's outline, borders |
| Blue, softer | `#5b6a9c` | secondary text |
| **Red pen** | `#cf3a2d` | labels, marks, the main button, urgency |
| **Highlighter yellow** | `#ffe873` | emphasis, sticky notes |
| Highlighter pink | `#ffb9cf` | the selected state |
| Pencil grey | `#8d8778` | quiet, incidental text |

**Rules the current design follows, which the identity must follow too:**

1. **Three inks doing three jobs.** Ballpoint blue writes. Red pen marks and
   warns. Highlighter yellow emphasises. Do not add a fourth accent colour.
2. **Nothing is a perfect rectangle.** Every box has four *different* corner
   radii so no two corners match. That is what makes it read as drawn by a hand
   rather than generated by a computer. Keep this everywhere.
3. **No emoji, ever.** Not as icons, not as decoration. Every icon in the product
   is hand-drawn line art. Emoji were removed deliberately because they were the
   loudest "made by a machine" signal in the design.
4. **Lines look drawn, not plotted.** Slightly uneven weight, ends that overshoot
   the corner a little, a wobble in a long stroke. Confident, not shaky.
5. **The doll is a soft plush voodoo doll, drawn by hand.** A big round head with
   two solid dot eyes, worried little brows, pink cheek blush and a small smile; a
   chubby bean-shaped body with a stitched seam down the tummy; stubby arms and
   legs. All of it in ballpoint blue line with a plush off-white fill, hanging
   from a thin string. He keeps the faint smile even while being hit — that
   contrast is most of the joke. He is drawn on graph paper, not ruled paper.
6. **Typography is handwriting.** Three hands are already in use: a rounded print
   hand for body text, a looser script for the tagline, and a thick marker for
   the logo word. Match that spirit; do not introduce a geometric sans.

## What I need you to make

### 1. The wordmark

The word **beatass**, set as one word, no space. Today it is marker-pen
lettering, in which "beat" is ballpoint blue and "ass" is red pen. Keep that
two-colour split — it is the whole joke of the name and it stops the word reading
as one crude thing.

Give me:
- **Horizontal lockup** — the doll icon to the left of the word. This is the
  in-app header and the email header.
- **Stacked lockup** — the doll above the word, centred. For narrow spaces.
- **Wordmark alone** — no icon.
- **One-colour version** — for when only a single ink is available. Solid
  ballpoint blue, and a version in white for dark backgrounds.

### 2. The icon (the doll's head)

A mark that works at 16 pixels. The full doll will not survive that, so I need
his head alone: the big round circle, the two solid dot eyes, the pink cheek
blush, the little smile, and the string coming off the top. Ballpoint blue line
on paper cream. If the blush muddies at tiny sizes, drop it there.

Give me:
- **Full detail** — for 512px and up.
- **Simplified** — thicker strokes, fewer marks, still recognisably him, for
  32px and 16px. Test it at 16px before you call it done; if the eyes merge into
  the smile, simplify further.
- **On a solid fill** — the head in cream on a red-pen circle, for places that
  crop everything to a circle.

### 3. Favicon set

- `favicon.svg` — the simplified head, scalable.
- 32×32 and 16×16 PNG.
- 180×180 PNG for the iPhone home screen — full-bleed, no transparency, paper
  cream background, the head centred with generous margin.
- A monochrome version for Safari's pinned-tab strip.

### 4. Email header

A strip **560 pixels wide and about 90 tall** that sits at the top of the emails
we send. Horizontal lockup, paper cream background, and a hint of the red margin
rule down the left edge so it matches the email body underneath it.

Please supply it as a **PNG at 2× (1120×180)** as well as SVG — mail clients will
not render an SVG.

Also: a **dark-mode variant**. Gmail and Apple Mail invert backgrounds, and cream
paper turning into dark grey with blue ink on it is unreadable.

### 5. Instagram

We are opening an account for this.

- **Profile picture, 1080×1080.** It will be cropped to a circle and shown at
  about 40px, so it has to survive that. My instinct is the head on a red-pen
  circle with the paper texture behind, but show me two or three directions.
- **Three story-highlight covers**, same style, for: *how it works*, *the best
  ones*, *is this real*.
- **One 1080×1350 post template** — the tall format. Paper background, ruled
  lines, room for a short handwritten line of text and the doll. We will drop
  different messages into it, so leave the text area clearly defined.

### 6. Open Graph image

**1200×630**, the picture that appears when the link is shared on WhatsApp,
Twitter, Slack. It needs to say what the product is in under a second: the doll,
the wordmark, and the line *"say the thing you'd never say"*. There is an
existing one — treat it as a first attempt to beat, not as a constraint.

## Deliverables

- SVG for everything that can be vector.
- PNG at 1× and 2× for everything that touches email or social.
- One board showing every mark at its real size, including the 16px favicon, so
  I can see what actually survives.
- The colour values you used, so I can check them against the table above.

## What I do not want

- Gradients, drop shadows that look like glass, glow, or bevel. The only shadow
  in the product is a hard offset one, like a sticker lifted off the page.
- A polished startup logo. If it looks like it came from a brand agency, it is
  wrong.
- A corporate mascot. He is cute, but homemade-cute — a small stuffed doll
  someone drew by hand in a lesson — never Pixar-cute. No mascot poses, no
  glossy rendering, no giant sparkly eyes. Dot eyes, wobbly line, done.
- Anything that reads as violent or genuinely nasty. He gets hit and he keeps
  smiling. Menace kills it.
- Emoji. Not once.

## How I will judge it

Someone sees the icon on their phone home screen and, without reading the name,
half-remembers it as *the little guy from that site*. Someone gets the email and
believes a person made this rather than a company. That is the bar.
