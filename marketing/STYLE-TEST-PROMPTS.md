# Style bake-off: copy-paste prompts for Google Flow

Created 2026-08-03. One identical test beat rendered in 8 different styles so they can be compared honestly. Paste each prompt into Flow (select a Veo mode that shows AUDIO, pick 9:16 vertical, generate 2 takes per prompt). Judge each clip full screen ON A PHONE with these five questions:

1. Muted test: do you get the joke with sound off?
2. Intentional test: does it look designed, or like AI slop?
3. Character test: does the doll feel like someone you would follow?
4. Loop test: would it replay cleanly?
5. Tone test: darkly funny, not disturbing, not cute-only?

The test beat held constant across all styles: a note gets pinned to the doll, an oversized soft fist bops it, the doll wobbles and gives the camera a deadpan look.

The audio ladder (Sanjay's call, 2026-08-03): test clips use Flow's built-in audio -> switch narration to the local Voice Box model (license check required before anything publishes with it) -> later a cloned real voice -> paid voice tool only if videos show traction.

## 1. Living notebook (the brand style)

```text
Vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. A 2D hand-drawn animation on lined notebook paper: a
small voodoo doll sketched in blue ballpoint pen sits at the center of the page.
A red-pen arrow draws itself pointing at the doll, a paper note gets pinned to
its chest, then a big doodle fist bops it once and the doll wobbles like jelly
and stares at the camera deadpan. Rough sketchy linework that boils and jitters
like a flipbook, yellow highlighter accents, visible paper texture and ruled
lines. Fixed camera, centered subject, empty top third of the page. Audio:
pencil scribble, one comic thump, a small paper rustle. No music, no dialogue,
no captions, no logos, no readable words anywhere.
```

## Style routing by story tone (Sanjay's rule, 2026-08-03)

The style is chosen PER STORY by its tone, one motion sheet per style:

| Story tone | Style |
|---|---|
| Dark / mystery / crime | Graphic-novel noir (sheet: NOIR-CONFESSION) or Dark storybook |
| Romantic / warm / bittersweet | Stop-motion miniature |
| Petty / funny / chaotic | Living notebook or Paper cutout zine |
| Brainrot filler layer | Doll endless-runner |

Vox documentary collage: skipped as too common (his call). STORY-001 is locked to Graphic-novel noir.
