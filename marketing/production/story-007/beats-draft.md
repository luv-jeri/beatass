# Story 007 Beats Draft

Visual style lock for every clip: marker pop on ruled notebook paper, loose hand-drawn outlines, vivid saturated marker colors, exaggerated huge-eyed expressions, and fast readable motion. Fixed protagonist description, reused verbatim in every action: "the same huge-eyed young adult with short black hair, a mustard hoodie, black jeans, and white socks". Keep the action inside one apartment living room at continuous night; carry the deep-blue window light and amber floor-lamp light through Beats 1-8, add a rising green phone glow from Beats 2-8, and let only that phone glow die in Beat 9. Any readable phone message is clean composited diegetic text, not generated label text.
