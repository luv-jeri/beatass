What beatass.com stores when you send a message, what it never stores, and how to make it stop.

Privacy

Last updated 2 August 2026.

This page describes exactly what beatass.com does with information. It is written to be checked against the code, not to be long.

There is no account and no tracking

beatass.com has no sign-up, no login, and no password. It sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising scripts, and no third-party trackers of any kind. The page you use is a single file that runs entirely in your own browser until the moment you press Send.

What happens before you press Send

Everything - the doll, the recording, the GIF and the video - is made on your own device. Nothing is uploaded while you are writing or while you are taking it out on the doll. If you close the tab before sending, nothing ever leaves your computer.

What we store when you do press Send

  • The recipient's name and email address, so the message can be delivered.
  • Your message.
  • A short summary of what you did to the doll, such as the number of hits.
  • The GIF and the video, stored as files.
  • The time it was sent.
  • A one-way scrambled version of your IP address. Not the address itself.

What we never store

Nothing that identifies you as the sender. No name, no email address, no account, no raw IP address. Your IP address is passed through a one-way function with a secret key, and only a shortened version of the result is kept. We cannot look your address up from it. It can only be used to tell whether two messages came from the same place, which is there for two narrow reasons: to stop one person flooding the site, and so that a message reported as abusive can be matched to others from the same source. We do not attempt to reverse it, and we do not build any way to.

Anonymity here is permanent. We do not reveal who sent a message, we do not hint at it, and we do not sell hints about it - not to the recipient, not to anyone. There is no paid tier that unmasks a sender, and there never will be.

Who else sees this

The site runs on Cloudflare, which stores the files and the database and handles the traffic. Email is delivered by Resend. Both are service providers acting on our behalf and both are bound by their own terms. No other company receives this information, and it is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

If you received a message and want it to stop

Every email carries a block link. One click and that address is added to a permanent block list, checked before every single send, and nobody can use beatass.com to reach that exact address again. There is no undo, on purpose: the person who blocked us is not the person who would be asking to reverse it. One limit worth knowing: the block matches the address as written, so if your provider treats variations of it as the same inbox - the dots in a Gmail address, or anything after a plus sign - block the variation you were written to, and email us if another one still gets through.

Every email also carries a report link, which flags the message for a human to look at.

How long things are kept

Messages and their media are kept so that the block and report links in an email keep working, and so a report can actually be investigated. To have a message and its media deleted, write to someone@beatass.com with the report link from the email, or the address it was sent to.

Children

beatass.com is for people aged 18 and over. It is not intended for children and we do not knowingly keep information from anyone under 18.

Questions
