# @_beatAss_ Instagram Follower-Growth Playbook

## Goal and positioning

**Niche and positioning:** BeatAss is the hand-drawn notebook account for fictional confession stories, unsent thoughts, and free anonymous messages for adults who have something they cannot say directly.

This is a short-life, viral-or-die growth plan. Make original, clearly labeled fictional entertainment and invite real people to use the real product. Never manufacture a message, testimonial, screenshot, reaction, follower count, or result.

## Account SEO and profile conversion

Fable's current findings say that Instagram search now gives the name field, username, bio keyword cluster, and clear profile promise unusual weight. Keep one repeated semantic cluster across the name field, bio, on-screen hooks, captions, and alt text: `anonymous message`, `confession`, `secret`, `unsent texts`, and `say it anonymously`.

### Name-field options

All options are under Instagram's 64-character limit.

1. `BeatAss | Anonymous Messages & Confessions`
2. `BeatAss | Send Anonymous Confessions`
3. `BeatAss | Secrets, Unsent Texts, Confessions`

**Recommendation:** use option 1. It keeps the brand first and covers the strongest search cluster without sounding like a generic confession page.

### Bio options

All options are under Instagram's 150-character limit.

**A. Active-outreach bio**

```text
CHECK YOUR DM REQUESTS
Someone sent an anonymous message.
Confessions and secrets, free at beatass.com
```

**B. SEO-first bio**

```text
Anonymous messages, confessions, and secrets.
Say what you cannot say. Free at beatass.com
```

**C. Story-led bio**

```text
Fictional confession stories + anonymous messages.
Say the unsent thing, free: beatass.com
```

### Bio A/B operating rule

This is an operating A/B, not a random daily bio swap.

| Situation | Live bio | Recommendation | What to compare weekly |
|---|---|---|---|
| The disclosed DM-requests outreach notifier is actively running | A | Required. The hook tells notified people where Instagram put their real message. | Profile visits from outreach, follows, safe replies, and any account-limit warning. |
| Outreach is paused or growth SEO is the priority | B | Recommended default. It explains the product in searchable language in about three seconds. | Search-led profile visits, profile-to-follow rate, link clicks, and follows per Reel reach. |
| Story series is the primary campaign for a defined week | C | Use only as a deliberate story-series test. It trades some SEO clarity for a sharper entertainment promise. | Follows per story-Reel reach and Story 002 completion. |

**Sanjay makes the final call.** Record the start and finish date, live bio, name field, outreach state, and baseline metrics before each 14-day comparison. Do not change name field, content cadence, or outreach caps inside a test. When the notifier is live, do not use B or C: the DM-requests instruction is the product truth the notified person needs.

### Three-second profile check

A visitor should immediately understand:

1. This is about anonymous messages, confessions, secrets, and unsent texts.
2. The public stories are fictional entertainment, never received user messages.
3. They can use the free real product at beatass.com.

Pin these three posts once available: a short "what BeatAss is" Reel, Story 001 Part 1, and the strongest saveable carousel. Use highlights only for `Stories`, `How it works`, and `Safety` when there is real material to put in them.

### Reel construction rules

1. Put the premise in the first 1.5 seconds. A stranger should know whether the Reel is about a secret, an unsent text, a roommate, a relationship, or a fictional story before the scroll decision.
2. Change the visual beat at least five times in the first five seconds: a red annotation, highlighted phrase, doodle movement, crop change, doll action, or subtitle change. This is the notebook-style version of Jade Beason's five-elements rule.
3. For story Reels, use the established 30 to 35 second arc: hook, escalation, doll payoff, verdict bait, then a short beatass.com end card.
4. Use clean, original 1080x1920 masters. Do not reuse watermarked clips or fake a received message.
5. Caption each fictional Reel with: `Fictional skit. beatass only sends messages written by real people. Keep it funny, not cruel.`

## Daily engagement routine

Spend 20 to 30 focused minutes per day. Genuine niche participation matters more than a broad like spree.

| Minutes | Action | Limit and quality bar |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Find 5 to 10 adult-audience accounts in relationship humor, roommate humor, friendship humor, original meme, confession, or hand-drawn storytelling. | Exclude school, youth, humiliation, revenge, and abuse accounts. |
| 10 | Leave specific comments on original recent posts. | Maximum 15 comments per day total, including any configured notifier comments. Reference a real detail in the post. No generic praise, link drops, or copied comments. |
| 5 | Follow qualified accounts where the fit is real. | Maximum 25 follows per day total. Spread them through the session. Never follow to bait a reply. |
| 5 to 10 | Reply to real comments on @_beatAss_, save high-signal hook examples, and log one lesson. | Reply only when useful and safe. Never DM commenters for acquisition. |

If a platform warning, account limit, opt-out, or negative response appears, stop the relevant action that day and log it. The disclosed notifier remains for recipients of real BeatAss messages only. It is not a growth cold-DM channel.

## Collab plan

### Accounts to pitch

Prioritize accounts roughly two to three times @_beatAss_'s current size when they meet all of these conditions:

- Original relationship, friendship, roommate, work, or harmless-petty humor.
- Adult and broadly relatable audience with active, moderated comments.
- A recent Reel proves they still publish original short-form content.
- Their bio, contact page, or recent post explicitly invites collaborations, pitches, submissions, or DMs for that purpose.
- Their style can support a clearly fictional notebook skit or a shared carousel. No child, school, revenge, harassment, or public-shaming angles.

Good targets are small original meme pages, confession-story pages that label fiction, relationship and humor creators, illustrated notebook creators, and creator couples who make situational comedy. Pitch a specific original idea that fits their recent work. Offer a collab post only after they choose to participate, and do not claim partnership until they accept in writing.

### Pitch message

Use only where the account publicly welcomes collaboration pitches or submissions. Personalize the bracketed detail with a true fact. One message, no automatic follow-up, no account access request, and no posting obligation.

```text
Hi [Name], I am [Your name], the maker of beatass.com.

I liked your recent [specific post] because [real detail]. BeatAss makes fictional notebook-style confession stories and a free tool for sending a real anonymous message.

I have a fictional [roommate / group-chat / unsent-text] Reel idea that could fit your style. It would be original, clearly labeled fictional, and made as a clean 9:16 collab post if you like it. No payment, no posting obligation, and no account access needed.

Want the one-paragraph concept? If not, no problem and no follow-up.
```

## Weekly scorecard and decisions

Track the account's own rolling median. A tiny account needs directional learning, not celebrity benchmarks.

| Metric | Why it matters | Weekly decision |
|---|---|---|
| Net new followers and follows per post | The primary goal is follower growth, not vanity views. | Rank every post by followers gained. Repeat the top two underlying topics. |
| Non-follower reach and profile visits | Shows whether content is creating new discovery. | If reach rises but follows do not, tighten name field, bio, cover, and end card. |
| Profile-to-follow rate | Tests the three-second profile promise. | Compare by bio mode, not by a single lucky post. |
| Average watch time, 3-second hold, and completion | Tests first-frame clarity and story pacing. | Replace the first 1.5 seconds after five weak comparable Reels. |
| Shares and saves per reach | The strongest content-quality signals in the supplied Fable findings. | Turn the most-shared premise into a carousel, quickie, and story prompt. |
| Comments per reach and safe-comment rate | Measures conversation without rewarding abuse. | Retire prompts that attract targeting, cruelty, or low-quality dogpiles. |
| Link clicks and site sessions per reach | Separates entertainment reach from product curiosity. | Match Reel CTA to the hook when views do not create clicks. |
| Qualified collab replies and accepted collabs | Measures borrow-audience work honestly. | Improve targeting after 25 qualified pitches with weak response. Never increase volume blindly. |

Every Monday, make only three decisions: keep two winning hooks, retire or rewrite two weak formats, and choose one next experiment. Record the exact hook, format, bio mode, hashtag set, publish time, and result so a later winner is repeatable.
