# Story 002 candidates - the dark slate (2026-08-04)

Built on the psychology Sanjay named and the research confirms: anger is the
strongest sharing trigger ever measured, fear and anxiety next, moral outrage
adds shares, satisfaction of justice makes people tag friends. Both sides
extreme: the injustice must make viewers furious, the payback must feel total.

One production rule that protects reach: Instagram deletes or throttles
explicit sexual content and graphic gore. So the camera looks away at the
worst moment - the audience's imagination does the work. Taboo in WHAT
happens (obsession, adultery, ruin), restraint in HOW it is shown. Every
reel opens with FICTIONAL STORY.

---

## A. The Wedding Gift (nuclear revenge)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** "My stepsister stole my fiance. So I bought her wedding."

**The injustice (Part 1):** Maya's stepsister slept with her fiance, then took
the wedding fund Maya's dying mother left her - and at the engagement party,
played a recording edited to make Maya sound insane. Her family chose the
stepsister. Maya lost the man, the money, the family, in one night.

**The turn (Part 2):** Maya goes silent for two years. Viewers watch her build
- night classes, a business, a shell company. The stepsister's dream venue,
the caterer, the photographer: every vendor the couple hires is quietly
Maya's. They plan their perfect wedding inside her trap and never notice.

**The revenge (Part 3):** At the vows, the venue's owner steps forward to
"say a few words about this couple." It is Maya - with the original,
unedited recording, and the paper trail showing whose money paid for it all.
She does not scream. She presses play. Verdict card: "Too far, or exactly
enough?"

**Why it spreads:** pure anger fuel, a two-year slow burn for retention, a
cathartic public reveal, and an ending people will fight about in comments.

## B. The Best Friend's Wife (obsession thriller)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** "She was married. So was he. She kept a notebook about
him anyway."

**The obsession (Part 1):** Riya has a good husband. But at her husband's
birthday dinner she meets his best friend - and something breaks loose. It
starts small: rereading his messages, finding reasons to visit. Then the
notebook: every detail of him, pages of it. The last line of Part 1: her
husband almost finds it.

**The escalation (Part 2):** A fake account to befriend the friend's wife.
Whispered lies that crack that marriage. "Accidental" meetings engineered
weeks ahead. She crosses lines that make viewers physically uncomfortable -
each one bigger - while playing the perfect wife at home.

**The collapse (Part 3):** The friend's marriage breaks. He comes to her
husband's house, shattered - and Riya is there, ready. But the notebook is
not where she left it. Her husband is holding it. Final card: "Who do you
feel sorry for?"

**Why it spreads:** taboo (double adultery, obsession), anxiety that climbs
every scene, and a shame-fascination that makes people send it to a friend
with "watch this whole thing."

## C. The Scholarship Kid (institutional revenge)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** "A teacher destroyed a poor kid's future. Ten years
later, the school called that kid... sir."

**The injustice (Part 1):** Mr. Khanna humiliated scholarship student Arjun
for years - mocked his shoes, his lunch, his accent, in front of everyone.
Final cruelty: a false cheating accusation before the board exam that cost
Arjun his university seat. The school believed the teacher. Arjun's father
apologized to the man who ruined his son.

**The build (Part 2):** Arjun rebuilt alone - repeated the year, built a
company. The school's roof leaks now; the trust is broke. A donor appears,
offering to save it - anonymous, until the signing ceremony.

**The revenge (Part 3):** Arjun signs, then makes one condition: the ethics
inquiry his 17-year-old self was denied - public, with the evidence he has
quietly collected from every other student Khanna broke. The teacher keeps
his pension only if he reads Arjun's expulsion letter aloud to the school.
Verdict card: "Mercy or weakness?"

**Why it spreads:** the deepest anger well (a child wronged by power),
huge relatability, and a revenge that is total without a drop of blood.

---

## Recommendation

Start with A (The Wedding Gift): anger + betrayal + a visual, public payoff -
the strongest match to the research and effortless to render in the notebook
style (venue maps, vendor lists, the recording doodle). B second - its
discomfort is the sharpest shock value we can run without triggering
moderation. C third as the series that builds the deepest loyalty.
