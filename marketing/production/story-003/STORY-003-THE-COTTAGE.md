# Story 003 — "The Cottage" (the paintings were windows)

Sanjay's story idea (2026-08-04), refined per reel-factory. Directed run: he
asked for end-to-end completion including the post, so the human gates for
script/VO/render are covered by his in-conversation instruction this time.

## Format decision: ONE reel

This is a twist story — the entire payoff is the last line ("they were
windows"). Splitting it strands the reveal in a part 2 most viewers never see.
A ~50s single reel puts the twist in the final seconds, which is also the
strongest shape for rewatches and shares. Multi-part stays the tool for
stories with real mid-point cliffhangers (story-001 had one; this does not).

## Story logic rules (from Sanjay, kept on purpose)

- At night the cottage is shown ONLY from the front: door, one lit window,
  black forest everywhere else. The side walls are never visible at night, so
  nobody can ask "why didn't he see the people outside".
- The reveal order: morning panic first, explanation after — quick flashback
  feel, then the two reveal lines.
- Fiction is disclosed: top label reads A CAMPFIRE STORY (this venture never
  passes fiction off as fact).
