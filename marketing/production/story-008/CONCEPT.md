# Story 008 Concept

## Dinner Was Waiting

A person comes home to an empty house and finds a warm dinner waiting on the kitchen table. A ticking timer leads them past locked, silent rooms, then rings from the table behind them. Back at the table, one covered plate begins pushing upward from underneath. The person lifts the cover and finds a meal with eyes shaped exactly like their own. Reasonable call: the two chosen hook clauses share one opening sentence because repeating "dinner" into sentence 2 creates a stronger continuity handoff than keeping the original period.

## CONTINUITY CHAIN

1 -> 2: Anchor word "dinner" repeats.
2 -> 3: Anchor words "room", "timer", and "table" repeat.
3 -> 4: Anchor word "table" repeats.
4 -> 5: Anchor phrase "covered plate" repeats.
5 -> 6: Cause word "So" links the pushing meal to lifting the cover.
6 -> 7: Cause word "Then" continues from the lifted cover.
7 -> 8: Anchor word "eyes" repeats.
