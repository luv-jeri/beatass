body plan & rigging
  • a puppet, not a noose — the string meets the back of the head, tied off with a red stitch; the body hangs at a 3° tilt, clearly alive.
  • Big round head, solid dot eyes, blush at 30% #e0507f; bean body with a red stitched seam; the tummy is otherwise clean — bruises, pins and kisses are painted on at runtime.
  • Limbs are separate closed shapes that connect under the body, each group carrying a data-pivot for swing: string · head · blush · body · stitches · arm-left · arm-right · leg-left · leg-right · face-*
  • Every state sits on the identical viewBox 0 0 150 210 — faces swap without anything shifting. Master file with all six face groups: svg/doll-master.svg; one file per state beside it.
  • Flat colour and wobbly pen line only — no gradients, no shadows, no filters. Outline 3.9px ballpoint #26356e, plush fill #fffdf5.
