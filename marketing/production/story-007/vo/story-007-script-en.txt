I deleted the text. Then it replied. Then the reply showed a photo of me holding the phone beside my apartment window. The window was black, so I turned and saw only my reflection holding that phone. That reflection raised its phone while my hand stayed still, so my phone buzzed. The phone displayed another text because the reflection had typed, "Do not turn around." So I froze. Then the reflection smiled at my phone. The phone went dark; the reflection kept typing.
