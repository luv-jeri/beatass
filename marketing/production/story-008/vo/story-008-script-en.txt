The house was empty, but dinner was still warm. The dinner waited on my kitchen table beside a ticking timer, so I checked every locked room. Every room was silent, then the timer rang from the table behind me. The table held one covered plate, so I stepped closer through the kitchen. The covered plate rattled because the meal underneath was pushing upward. So I lifted the cover. Then the meal opened eyes shaped exactly like mine. My eyes blinked; I did not.
