what makes him hittable
  • The boil. Every stroke drawn twice with a wobble re-rolled every 110ms — about nine times a second. This is the single most important animation in the product.
  • A face that acts. He blinks on his own schedule, his eyes track about, his brows ride up when he's worried, and he flinches before you've even touched him.
  • Weight on impact. Squash, recoil, screen shake, a comic-book word, and a burst of debris.
  • Damage that stays. Every other punch leaves a permanent mark — bruise hatching, a plaster, a scratch. They ride on him and swing with him.
  • A drawn invitation. A pulsing dashed ring and "hit him", gone the instant he's touched.
