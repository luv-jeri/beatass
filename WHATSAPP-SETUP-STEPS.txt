WHATSAPP SETUP - WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
====================================

Everything the computer could do is done. What is left is on your phone,
plus a few one-word replies back in the chat.

There are 5 steps. Step 1 is on your phone (about 3 minutes).
Steps 2 to 5 are one word each in the chat.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - SET UP THE BUSINESS PROFILE (on your phone, 3 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Why: a stranger who gets a message from an unknown business number decides
in about two seconds whether to block it. The profile is the first thing
they check. It has to say plainly that we only deliver the message, and
that reporting exists.

Open WhatsApp Business on your phone.

Everything below is written to be pasted EXACTLY as it is, emoji included.
The emoji are not decoration - they are what stops the message reading as
a grey wall of text that nobody finishes.


1a. THE ABOUT LINE
    Tap: Settings  ->  tap your own name at the top  ->  About
    Delete "At the gym" and paste this instead (129 characters, fits):

📩 we only deliver anonymous messages from beatass.com. we did not write them. every message has a link to reply, report or block.


1b. THE DESCRIPTION
    Tap: Settings  ->  Business tools  ->  Business profile  ->  Description
    Paste this (244 characters, fits):

📩 beatass.com lets someone write an anonymous message and take it out on a voodoo doll. ⚠️ we only deliver it - we never write the words and never share your number. 🛑 every message has a link to read it, reply, report it, or block us for good.


1c. WEBSITE
    Same screen, the Website field:

https://beatass.com


1d. EMAIL
    Same screen, the Email field:

someone@beatass.com


1e. CATEGORY
    Same screen. Choose "Internet company", or the closest thing on the list.


1f. THE GREETING MESSAGE  (this is the important one)
    Tap: Settings  ->  Business tools  ->  Greeting message
    Turn it ON, set it to send to "Everyone", and paste this as the message
    (190 characters, fits under WhatsApp's 200 limit):

👋 *you have not messaged a person.* this is beatass.com - we only deliver anonymous messages, we did not write yours. 👉 the link in it lets you *reply*, *report* it, or *block us for good* 🛑

    The stars around words are not a mistake. WhatsApp turns *like this*
    into bold when the message is sent, so the important words stand out.

    Why this step matters most: the greeting fires automatically the first
    time somebody replies to us. It answers the question they are actually
    asking ("who is this and why do you have my number?") before they
    reach for Block. WhatsApp on a computer cannot set this at all, only
    the phone app can, which is why this whole step is on your phone.

    IMPORTANT: do not change your profile NAME ("Beat Ass"). The sending
    tool checks that name to be sure it is signed in as the right account,
    and it refuses to send if it does not match. If you ever do change it,
    tell me and I will update the setting on the laptop.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - SAY "DEPLOY" IN THE CHAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

What I will do: add the new WhatsApp column to the live database and put
the new version of the site online.

Why it is needed: right now beatass.com has no WhatsApp field on it. Every
test so far ran against a copy of the database on your laptop. Until this
happens, nobody can type a WhatsApp number on the real site.

This is your decision because it changes the live site and the live
database. Nothing happens until you say it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - TEST IT LIKE A REAL PERSON (2 minutes, you)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Open beatass.com on your phone or laptop. Write a confession, put YOUR OWN
WhatsApp number in the new field, hit the doll, and send it.

Then tell me, and I will deliver it. You should get three messages, and
this time the link will actually open, because the message will really
exist on the live site. Click the link. Try the block button on that page.
That is the whole feature working end to end.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - SAY "ARM THE TIMER"
--------------------------------------------------------------------

What I will do: install a small background job that wakes up every 60
seconds, looks for messages waiting to be sent, and sends them without
anybody watching.

What you will notice: when there IS something to send, a WhatsApp window
opens on your screen by itself, sends the messages, and closes. That looks
odd but it is necessary - WhatsApp refuses to stay logged in unless there
is a real visible window (I tested this twice). When nothing is waiting,
no window ever opens, so most of the time you will never see it.

Off switch, any time, one line:
launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.beatass.whatsapp.plist


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - SAY "COMMIT"
--------------------------------------------------------------------

I save all of this work to the project history. Nothing is saved yet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG LATER
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The full technical version of all of this lives in WHATSAPP.md in the
project folder. When WhatsApp changes its website and the sender breaks,
it stops and saves a photo of the screen it was stuck on, at:

tools/whatsapp/last-failure.png

Show me that photo and it is usually a one-line fix.
