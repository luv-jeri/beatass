Why beatass.com exists, how the anonymous confession and the voodoo doll work, and the lines it will not cross.

About beatass

Most of the things people most need to say never get said. The thank you that felt too sincere. The apology that arrived years late in your head and never made it out of it. The sentence you have rehearsed in the shower for a decade. beatass.com exists for exactly those sentences.

How it works

You write the thing. You take it out on a hand-drawn doll — punch him, pin him, set him on fire, or love him, whichever the feeling actually is. The site records what you did as a short animation while you are doing it. Then it emails your words and the recording to the person you named, and never tells them who sent it.

The whole page is a single file that runs in your browser. The doll's physics, the drawing, the recording and the video are all made on your own device. Nothing is uploaded until you press Send.

Why the doll

Because a bare anonymous message is a nasty little object, and this is not meant to be one. Beating the doll makes the feeling visible and slightly ridiculous at the same time, which is the difference between a confession and a threat. It is why the loving version exists too, and why it is one of the four tools rather than a hidden option.

The lines this will not cross

Anonymous messaging apps have a well-documented way of going wrong, and the two worst versions are both banned here permanently:

  • We never hint at who sent something. Not for money, not as a feature, not as a joke. Selling "hints" about senders is the thing that turned a well-known competitor into a five-million-dollar fine.
  • We never fake a message. Not to make the site look busy, not to bring somebody back, not once. Every message on beatass.com was written by a real person.
  • Every email carries a block link that works in one click and lasts forever, and a report link that puts the message in front of a human.

Who made it

One person, as a small independent project. It is free, it carries no advertising, and it has no investors asking it to grow in the directions that ruin things like this.

Questions people actually ask

Will they find out it was me?

No. beatass.com never reveals who sent a message, never hints at it, and never sells hints about it. No name, email address or raw IP address is stored for the sender.

Is beatass free?

Yes. It is free, there is no sign-up, no account and no advertising.

What does the recipient actually get?

An email with your message written out in full, plus the animation of what you did to the doll, and two links: one to report the message and one to block their address permanently.

How do I stop receiving these?

Click the block link at the bottom of the email. It takes one click and it is permanent: that address is checked before every send and can never be contacted through beatass.com again.
