The rules for using beatass.com: who may use it, what you may not send, and what happens when someone breaks that.

Terms

Last updated 2 August 2026.

Using beatass.com means agreeing to what follows. It is short because the rules are short.

You must be 18 or over

beatass.com is for adults. If you are under 18, do not use it.

What this is

You write something you would not say out loud, you take it out on a drawing of a doll, and the site emails your words and the recording to the person you named. Your identity is never attached to it and never revealed.

What you may not send

The anonymity here exists so people can finally say a true thing. It does not exist to let anyone hide while doing harm. Do not use beatass.com to send:

  • Threats of violence, or anything that could reasonably be read as one.
  • Harassment, or repeated messages to somebody who does not want them.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18, in any form.
  • Content that reveals somebody's private information.
  • Anything illegal where you are, or where the recipient is.
  • Bulk or automated messages of any kind.
They never asked for this. Send the thing you would still be glad you sent tomorrow.

Limits that are enforced automatically

Sending is limited to five messages an hour from one place. Addresses that have blocked us are checked before every send and are never contacted again. Oversized files are refused. Oversized files are refused.

What we can do

We can refuse a message, remove a message and its media, block an address from being contacted, and block a sender, at our discretion and without notice. Reported messages are looked at by a human. Where the law requires it, we will comply with a valid legal request.

No warranty

beatass.com is provided as it is, with no guarantee that it will be available, that a message will arrive, or that it will arrive in an inbox rather than a spam folder. It is free, it is small, and it can break. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from using it, and nothing here creates a duty to keep the service running.

Your words

What you write stays yours. By sending it you give us permission to store it and deliver it to the person you named, which is the entire point, and nothing more than that.
