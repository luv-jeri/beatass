# Story 007 Concept

## The Deleted Reply

A person alone in an apartment deletes a text, and the deleted text immediately replies. The reply shows a photo taken from inside the apartment, with the person holding the same phone beside the window. The window reflection moves before the person does, then types a warning not to turn around. The person freezes while the reflection smiles and keeps typing after the real phone goes dark. Reasonable call: the reply in sentence 2 points directly at its own contents, so the photo introduces the phone and window without breaking the location arc.

## CONTINUITY CHAIN

1 -> 2: Cause word "Then" links deleting the text to its reply.
2 -> 3: Cause word "Then" continues directly from the impossible reply.
3 -> 4: Anchor words "window" and "phone" repeat.
4 -> 5: Anchor words "reflection" and "phone" repeat.
5 -> 6: Anchor words "phone" and "reflection" repeat.
6 -> 7: Cause word "So" links the warning to the freeze.
7 -> 8: Cause word "Then" continues forward from the freeze.
8 -> 9: Anchor words "phone" and "reflection" repeat.
