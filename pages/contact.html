How to reach beatass.com — to report a message, block your address, ask for a deletion, or say hello.

Contact

One address for everything: someone@beatass.com

It is read by a person, not a bot, and there is no ticket number.

If you received a message you did not want

You do not need to email anybody. The message itself has two links at the bottom:

  • Block my address forever — one click, permanent. That address is checked before every send from then on, so nobody can use beatass.com to reach it again. It matches the address as written, so if a variation of yours still gets through — Gmail ignores dots, and anything after a plus sign — block that one too, or write to us.
  • Report this — one click, and the message is flagged for a human to look at.

If the message was threatening, or if you think you know who sent it and you feel unsafe, contact your local police. We will comply with a valid legal request. Please also email someone@beatass.com with the report link so it gets attention faster.

To have a message deleted

Email someone@beatass.com with either the report link from the email you received, or the address it was sent to. The message and its media will be removed.

What we cannot tell you

We cannot tell you who sent a message. Not a name, not a city, not a hint, not for any amount of money. That is not a policy we are willing to make an exception to, because a promise of anonymity with exceptions is not a promise. The Privacy page explains what is and is not stored.

Anything else

Bugs, press, a feature that would make it better, or a complaint about the whole idea — the same address works. someone@beatass.com. See also About and Terms.
