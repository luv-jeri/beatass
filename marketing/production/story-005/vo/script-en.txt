I stood up in the middle of my best friend's wedding and I screamed, stop the wedding. Her mother slapped me. Her brothers dragged me out of the hall while the whole room booed at me, and the bride, my best friend since we were nine years old, told me she never wanted to see my face again. For two years, I was the jealous friend who could not watch her be happy. Then last month, she showed up at my door, shaking. She had found what I found. A second phone, a second city, a second family. A wife, and a little boy with his eyes. I knew it on her wedding day. Nobody believed me then. So before you call someone the villain of a story, ask yourself one question. What if they were the only one telling the truth?
