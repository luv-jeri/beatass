In 2013, a man confessed to a murder inside a meme. Reddit had a running joke called Confession Bear, a sad little bear you pinned your secrets to. The secrets were small and silly, like farting and blaming the dog, and everyone laughed along. Then one night at two in the morning, a user called Narado posted his own. His post said his sister's boyfriend had been violent and hooked on drugs, so he ended the man's life, and nobody ever knew. The death was ruled an overdose, and he titled the post, I finally have the guts to say it. Within hours it had ten thousand upvotes and three thousand comments, every one of them asking, is this a joke? Then Narado himself replied, with one single sentence.
