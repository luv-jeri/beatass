# STORY 001 - The Confession Bear Murder (3-part reel series)

Created 2026-08-03. Source: first confession in "Reddit's Most Disturbing Confessions" (youtube.com/watch?v=Hhcabi6IvBM, transcript at research/transcripts/v1.txt, 00:41-03:30). This is a REAL, widely documented 2013 internet event.

## Framing rules (non-negotiable)

- This is told as TRUE INTERNET HISTORY. On-screen label in part 1: `TRUE STORY - 2013`. It is never presented as a beatass submission.
- We use the Reddit username only. The man's real name and face were published by blogs at the time, but we do NOT use them. The username is the character. This costs zero virality and avoids dragging a real person's name up again.
- Stick to the documented record as retold in the source: the post, the upvotes, his one comment, the identification, the joke claim + deletion, the FBI emails, the cold case that produced no arrest, the sister calling it "an ill-advised prank." No new accusations, no invented details about the real people.
- No gore, no depiction of the death. The story is the MYSTERY, not the act.
- Characters are stylized and faceless: the poster is a hooded figure lit by a monitor; crowds are silhouettes and glowing speech bubbles; the bear is OUR OWN drawn sad bear (never the actual meme photo - that photograph is copyrighted).
- The doll appears ONLY on the final end card (its shrug + beatass.com). Human-scale story carries the series.

## Series shape

3 parts, 35-40 seconds each. Parts 1 and 2 end on cliffhangers, part 3 ends on verdict bait + CTA. Each part opens with a 2-second recap-hook so it works standalone (most viewers enter mid-series). Post 1 day apart. Pin a comment on part 1: "Part 2 is up" when it posts.

Assembly per part: 4 generated 8s scenes + hook card + end card, VO over the top, captions word-synced in the edit, music as directed below. All readable text (meme lines, labels, counters) is added in the EDIT, never generated.

Music direction: low dark ambient pulse throughout, one notch tenser each part. Part 3 drops to near-silence at the sister's answer, then one music-box note under the end card (the brand sound).

**VO script (about 100 words, read in ~30s):**

Then one night, a user called Narado posted his own. Top line: my sister's boyfriend was violent and on drugs. Bottom line: so I ended his life, and nobody ever knew. It was ruled an overdose. He titled it "finally have the guts to say it." Ten thousand upvotes. Three thousand comments. Everyone asking: is this a joke? Then Narado himself replied. One sentence.

# PART 2 - "The hunt"

**Hook card (0-2s):** `He confessed to murder in a meme. Then said "some of it is true" and vanished.`

**VO script (about 100 words):**

A man confessed to murder in a meme, wrote "there is some truth behind it," and vanished. Reddit does not let that go. His account was anonymous. But every comment he had ever posted was public. Thousands of strangers started digging. A birthday here. A workplace there. A linked profile, a city, a photo. Piece by piece, they rebuilt him: twenty-four years old. San Diego. Within days his real name and face were on the blogs. And he was watching it happen. He posted one last message. "It was just a joke." Then he deleted everything. Too late. Someone had already emailed the FBI.

# PART 3 - "The answer"

**Hook card (0-2s):** `The police opened a cold case... because of a meme.`

**VO script (about 110 words):**

The police opened a cold case because of a meme. San Diego detectives quietly looked into it. Did a young man really die of an overdose that was not an overdose? The internet waited for the arrest. It never came. No charges. No statement. Nothing. Then a journalist did the one thing Reddit could not. She found his sister, and asked her straight: did a boyfriend of yours ever die? Her answer: "It's an ill-advised prank. No one I know has overdosed." So the whole thing was fake. Except... remember the one comment he wrote. "There is SOME truth behind it. I'm not saying what." So. Confession, or the darkest joke ever posted?
