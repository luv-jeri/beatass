The police opened a cold case because of a meme. San Diego detectives quietly began checking whether a young man who died of an overdose had really died of something else. The internet waited for an arrest, and it never came. There were no charges, and no statement, just silence. Then a journalist did the one thing Reddit could not do. She found his sister and asked her directly if a boyfriend of hers had ever died. Her answer was that it was an ill-advised prank, and that nobody she knew had overdosed. So the whole confession was fake. Except for the one comment he wrote before he vanished, that there is SOME truth behind it. So you tell me. A confession, or the darkest joke ever posted?
