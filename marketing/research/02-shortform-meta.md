## The useful mental model

Neither platform gives a public formula. Make a post a stranger understands with the sound off, make its payoff worth sending to one specific friend, and make the first second prove why they should stay. A follower count is not the gate: recommendation surfaces can show eligible content to non-followers. That is the small-account opportunity, not a promise of reach.

For beatass, the product recording is the differentiator. The best clips should show an obviously fictional situation, then the doll gets its consequence. Do not build the account on fake customer posts, fake inbox screenshots, or implied user-volume claims.

## What each platform has actually said

### Instagram Reels

- Instagram's public ranking explanation says recommendations use predictions about actions a person may take. Reels recommendations can reach people who do not follow the account, subject to recommendation eligibility. **From-memory, verify live:** [Instagram ranking explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained), accessed 2026-08-03.
- Instagram's professional guidance has recently described watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach as key practical signals for Reels. Treat this as creator guidance, not a complete ranking formula. **From-memory, verify in the Instagram Professional Dashboard before quoting:** [Instagram Best Practices](https://creators.instagram.com/best-practices/), accessed 2026-08-03.
- A send is especially useful for beatass because it means the joke had a named recipient in the viewer's head. Saves are useful when a post is a prompt list, a caption template, or a "send this to your group chat" idea. Do not chase empty comments or generic likes at the expense of a strong sendable joke.
- Check Account Status before assuming a distribution problem is creative. Recommendation eligibility and content restrictions are visible to professional accounts there. **From-memory, verify live:** [Instagram recommendation guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232), accessed 2026-08-03.

### TikTok

- TikTok says its For You recommendations use user interactions, video information, and user/device settings. Interactions such as watching in full or skipping can carry more weight than weaker settings signals. **From-memory, verify live:** [How TikTok recommends content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/for-you), accessed 2026-08-03.
- TikTok's own growth guidance recommends regular high-quality posting, analytics review, audience engagement, and creator collaboration. It also calls out Creator Search Insights. **From-memory, verify live:** [How to grow your audience](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/how-to-grow-your-audience), accessed 2026-08-03.
- For TikTok search, say and show the situation phrase a real person would search, then repeat it naturally in the caption. Examples: "anonymous confession to my roommate", "petty coworker confession", "voodoo doll breakup joke". Do not pack an unrelated keyword list into the caption. **From-memory, verify in the current Creator Search Insights UI:** [TikTok Creator Search Insights](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-search-insights), accessed 2026-08-03.

## What to make for a new account

### Format rules

- Start on the consequence, not the logo: show the doll already mid-punch, a big six-to-nine-word premise, or the final text message in frame 0.
- Land the premise in the first 0.0 to 1.5 seconds. A viewer should know the social situation before they decide whether to scroll.
- Use one joke and one emotional turn per clip. Setup, escalation, doll payoff, then a simple action line.
- Make the visual work muted: large, high-contrast text, captions for speech, and no key information only in the audio.
- Put the actual product or product-like doll footage inside the first two seconds. Generic AI footage is weak proof and easily becomes interchangeable "AI slop".
- Keep an open loop only when the payoff arrives. Do not make a multi-part story just to force a follow. Every post must make sense by itself.

### Length and cadence

There is no official universal ideal length. The following is an experiment plan, **From-memory, not a platform rule**:

| Test type | Starting length | Why | Starting cadence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Doll payoff loop | 7 to 12 seconds | A visual gag can replay cleanly | 3 per week |
| Fictional confession mini-story | 15 to 25 seconds | Enough time for a setup, twist, and payoff | 3 per week |
| Reply or stitch response | 10 to 20 seconds | Lets the original creator or commenter remain the hook | 1 to 2 per week when there is a real prompt |
| Prompt-list or "which one are you?" post | 12 to 18 seconds | More saveable than a one-off gag | 1 per week |

Start with one native vertical video a day for 30 days, cross-posted without another platform's watermark. Use Stories three to five times a week to show work-in-progress, polls, and repostable frames. After 10 posts, cut the weakest of the four format types; after 20, make sequels only to posts with unusually high sends or shares per reach. This is a testing cadence, not an algorithm requirement.

### Captions, keywords, and hashtags

- Lead the caption with the situation phrase, not "new reel" or a generic CTA. Example: "When your roommate steals your oat milk for the fourth time..."
- Put the same natural phrase in on-screen text and, where appropriate, spoken audio. This gives both people and search systems a clear topic.
- Use three to five precise tags only when they describe the post: `#fictionalconfession`, `#roommatestories`, `#workhumor`, `#voodoodoll`, `#beatass`. Do not add `#fyp`, `#viral`, or an unrelated trending tag as a growth tactic.
- Keep the Instagram bio plain and searchable: "Fictional confessions meet a voodoo doll. Make one free at beatass.com." Do not claim "real messages" or user totals.
- Use TikTok Creator Search Insights to turn real search gaps into scripts. A search term is a premise, not a licence to copy another creator's video.

## Confession-story genre: use it without deception

The genre works because viewers instantly recognize a private social tension: a petty roommate, a terrible ex, a group-chat betrayal, a manager's odd habit, a crush, or a sincere apology. It can use POV captions, message bubbles, a deadpan voiceover, a game-like split screen, or deliberately over-the-top generated cutaways.

For beatass:

- Mark dramatized stories clearly: on-screen `FICTIONAL CONFESSION` or `DRAMATIZED`, and repeat that in the caption where the framing could otherwise be mistaken for a user submission.
- Use invented names and composite situations. Never scrape, recreate, or imply access to a real person's private messages.
- If a realistic synthetic person or event is generated, apply each platform's current AI-content disclosure. TikTok requires labelling some realistic AI-generated content; Meta can label AI-generated content from metadata or disclosure. **From-memory, verify before posting:** [TikTok AI-generated content policy](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/ai-generated-content) and [Meta manipulated media policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/manipulated-media/), accessed 2026-08-03.
- Skip the lazy "Subway Surfers underneath a stolen Reddit story" version. If split screen is tested, use original doll gameplay or a licensed/public-domain loop, and make the upper story visibly fictional.

## Hooks that fit the product

Use these as premise structures, not claims about real submissions:

1. `POV: your roommate says the milk "evaporated".`
2. `I wrote the apology. Then I chose fire.`
3. `The group chat has one anonymous truth left.`
4. `What do you send your ex at 2:14 a.m.?`
5. `This is why he is banned from the aux.`
6. `A fictional HR complaint, but make it arts and crafts.`
7. `Pick a pin: petty, honest, or fully unhinged.`
8. `Your situationship sent "u up?". The doll replied.`

The hook text should appear before or exactly with the first physical action. End with a low-pressure, disclosed invite: `Make your own fictional confession at beatass.com` or `What would your doll say?`.

## Distribution-safe creator tactics

GREEN:

- Reply to a genuine comment with a video when the reply is useful or funny.
- Invite a creator to use a fictional prompt and let them decline.
- Use duet/stitch only where the original creator has enabled it and the new work adds an actual joke or response.
- Credit the creator in the video/caption when their post was the direct prompt.
- Make a platform-native version with native text, captions, and audio rather than reposting a watermarked export.

AMBER, human decision:

- Test a currently trending sound only when its licence is available in the platform library and the joke genuinely fits.
- Respond to public brand/creator posts. Do it manually and individually; never create the impression of a partnership.
- Use a meme format. Confirm it does not use protected footage, a real private confession, or a harassment pile-on.

RED:

- Ask friends, employees, or bots to mass-comment, mass-save, or mass-send. It is fake engagement and risks enforcement.
- Claim a fictional story is a real beatass submission, or fabricate inbox screenshots/testimonials. It violates venture law and consumer-trust rules.
- Repost an unlicensed gameplay loop or another creator's full video as background filler.

## What to measure for each post

Measure by format and hook, not just total views:

- Hold: 3-second views divided by plays, where the platform reports it.
- Completion and average watch time, relative to the clip's length.
- Rewatches, if available, or average watch time above the length for a very short loop.
- Shares/sends per reach and saves per reach. These are the closest signals to a real spread loop.
- Profile visits per reach, link clicks, and completed product sessions. A viral clip that makes no new senders is entertainment, not acquisition.
- Negative signals: hides, reports, hostile comments, account-status restrictions, and link-blocking reports.

Kill a format after at least five comparable posts if it is below the account median on both hold and shares/sends per reach and has not created useful comments. Scale a format only after at least two posts show at least 2x the account median in shares/sends per reach, with no safety issue. This is a practical decision rule, **From-memory, not an official platform threshold**.
