My stepsister stole my fiance, so I bought her entire wedding. But first you need to see what she took from me. I came home early one Friday and found her in my bedroom, wearing my black dress, wrapped around the man I was going to marry. She didn't even stop. She just smiled at me over his shoulder. Then she took the wedding fund my dying mother left me, and at my own engagement party she played a recording, cut and spliced, that made me sound insane. My family believed her. My fiance chose her. I lost the man, the money, and my family in one night. So I went quiet. And I started planning.
