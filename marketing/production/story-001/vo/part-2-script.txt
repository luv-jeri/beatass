He confessed to a murder inside a meme, replied that there was some truth behind it, and then he vanished. But Reddit does not let a thing like that go. His account was anonymous, but every comment he had ever written was public, and thousands of strangers started digging through all of it. A birthday in one thread led to a workplace in another, then to a linked profile, a city, and finally a photo. Piece by piece they rebuilt him, and within days his real name and face were all over the blogs. He was watching it happen. So he posted one final message, claiming it was all a joke, and deleted everything. It was too late. Someone had already emailed the FBI.
