# Story 002 "The Wedding Gift" - Part 1 script options (GATE A: text approval)

Fictional revenge story (concept A from CONCEPTS-DARK.md). First frame + caption
carry `FICTIONAL STORY`. Three parts planned:

- Part 1: the betrayal - she loses the man, the money, and the family in one
  night. Ends on "I started planning."
- Part 2: two silent years - Maya builds, and every vendor the couple hires
  is secretly hers.
- Part 3: the wedding - the venue owner steps forward, presses play. Verdict
  card: "Too far, or exactly enough?"

Both options follow the ear rules: full connected sentences, one continuous
arc, no fragment stacks, no mid-sentence pauses, no emotion tags. Narrator:
Lily, first person (Maya). Read-aloud ~32-34s at her pace.

Every sentence below is written to be SHOWABLE - the words-match-video law.
The bracketed note after each line is what the screen will literally show
(not part of the script).

## Option A - the bedroom discovery (115 words)

My stepsister stole my fiance, so I bought her entire wedding.
[Maya at a window, wedding invitation in hand, slow smile]

But first you need to see what she took from me.
[notebook page turns, red pen underlines "what she took"]

I came home early one Friday and found her in my bedroom, wearing my black
dress, wrapped around the man I was going to marry.
[THE scene: bedroom, low light, the stepsister in the slipped-off-shoulder
black dress, arms around the fiance - sensual, no nudity, Maya frozen in
the doorway]

She didn't even stop.
[close on the embrace continuing]

She just smiled at me over his shoulder.
[the stepsister's slow smile straight into camera over his shoulder]

Then she took the wedding fund my dying mother left me, and at my own
engagement party she played a recording, cut and spliced, that made me
sound insane.
[hospital-bed envelope passing to the wrong hands; party room, phone on a
table playing, guests turning to stare at Maya]

My family believed her.
[family standing beside the stepsister, facing Maya]

My fiance chose her.
[his hand letting go of Maya's, taking the stepsister's]

I lost the man, the money, and my family in one night.
[Maya alone outside the party, three doodle icons crossing out: ring,
money, family photo]

So I went quiet.
[Maya's face going still, calm]

And I started planning.
[a fresh notebook page opening, her pen touching the first line - hard cut]

## Option B - the countdown (121 words)

Two years ago my stepsister slept with my fiance in my own bed, and on
Saturday I am paying for her wedding.
[split image: the bedroom silhouette / Maya signing a cheque, smiling]

She wanted everything that was mine.
[the stepsister trying on Maya's necklace in Maya's mirror]

I watched her lean into him at my engagement party, in a dress cut so low
he forgot I existed, and I told myself I was imagining it.
[the party: the stepsister in the deep-neckline dress leaning into the
fiance, his eyes locked on her, Maya watching across the room]

I was not imagining it.
[his hand at the small of the stepsister's back]

She took him, then she took the money my mother left me as she was dying,
and then she played my family a recording she had edited until I sounded
crazy.
[bedroom doorway scene; the envelope; the phone on the table, guests
staring]

They hugged her while I stood alone in the parking lot.
[the family circle around the stepsister through a window; Maya outside
alone under a streetlight]

She thinks I disappeared.
[Maya's photo removed from the family wall]

She is marrying him on Saturday, and every single thing she touches will
be mine.
[wedding planning board - venue, cake, photographer - and Maya's pen
circling every single vendor - hard cut]

## The ask (one word)

A or B. A opens on the punchier hook and gives the bedroom discovery its
full scene. B opens on the countdown and puts the deep-neckline party
moment center stage. My pick: A - the discovery scene is the anger fuel
this genre runs on, and the smile over the shoulder is the single most
shareable image in either script.
